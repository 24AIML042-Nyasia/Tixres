This folder acts as the entrypoint for all background tasks and scheduling, utilizing Celery and Redis.

Files and their contents:

1. app.py
- Initializes the Celery application (`myapp`) with a Redis backend/broker on localhost.
- Defines a `beat_schedule` to systematically run rollup algorithms and anomaly detection cron jobs:
  - one_min_roll_up: Every minute.
  - ten_min_roll_up: Every 10 minutes.
  - one_hour_roll_up: Every hour.
  - run_zscore_anomaly: Every 10 minutes.

2. tasks.py
Functions:
- one_min_rollup(), ten_min_rollup(), one_hour_rollup(): Celery task wrappers that initialize `InfluxDBRollup` and manage asyncio execution of rollup components.
- run_zscore_for_agent(agent_id, metrics): Instantiates the `ZScoreAnomaly` detector and delegates execution with retry properties (max_retries=3) explicitly.
- run_anomaly_cycle(): The primary anomaly distribution orchestrator. It fetches the required agents and metric mappings, forms a celery group of `run_zscore_for_agent` jobs, and enqueues them for parallel background asynchronous processing.
