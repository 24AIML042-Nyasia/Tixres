This folder governs ticketing operations derived from detected anomalies.

Files and their contents:

1. models.py
Classes:
- Ticket: SQLAlchemy domain class mapping the `tickets` relational table. Stores agent specific anomaly events including metric origin, severity, purpose, current status ('OPEN'), detector metadata, and a qualitative summary message.

2. ticketService.py
Classes & Functions:
- TicketService: Database service provider for orchestrating queries around `Ticket`.
  - create_ticket(agent_id, metric_name, severity, purpose, detector, meta, message): Writes an anomaly ticket into the database to notify administrators with purpose-aware deduplication.
  - get_Tickets(limit): Retrieves recent tickets from the database up to a specified limit.
